Introduction

Hello, my name is Namkhing. This website was created as a personal project to introduce myself and share the hobbies that are important to me.

I am interested in creative activities and storytelling, which is why most of my hobbies involve art, music, and series. These hobbies help me relax, express emotions, and understand different perspectives.

Through this website, I want visitors to understand who I am, what I enjoy, and why these activities play a meaningful role in my daily life.

About This Website

This website was created using HTML and CSS as part of a learning process to improve my web development skills. The layout uses a sidebar navigation and content sections to keep information organized and easy to read.

I focused on spacing, color balance, and structure to make the website look professional but not overly complex. Each page follows a consistent layout so users can navigate easily without confusion.

The content on this website may continue to grow in the future as I add more details, projects, and personal experiences.
