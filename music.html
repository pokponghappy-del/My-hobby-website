Why I Love Listening to Music

Music is an important part of my daily life. I listen to music when I feel tired, stressed, happy, or even when I need motivation. It helps me focus, calm my mind, and understand my emotions better.

Different types of music create different feelings. Sometimes I listen to soft songs to relax, and other times I enjoy energetic songs to boost my mood.

How I Started This Hobby

I started listening to music seriously when I was younger, mostly using my phone and headphones. At first, it was just for entertainment, but over time it became something meaningful to me.

Music slowly became something I rely on during study time, traveling, and before sleeping.

My Top 3 Favorite Artists

Polycat

Polycat is a Thai synth-pop band known for their retro 80s-inspired electronic sound. Their music combines catchy melodies with dreamy synths and smooth vocals, creating a nostalgic yet modern vibe that appeals to fans of upbeat pop and chill electronic tunes.This artist is one of my favorites because of their emotional songs and meaningful lyrics. Their music feels very personal and relatable.

Ariana Grande

Ariana Grande is an American pop and R&B singer recognized for her powerful vocal range and emotional delivery. Her music blends contemporary pop, R&B, and occasional dance elements, often featuring lush harmonies, catchy hooks, and themes of love, empowerment, and personal growth.I enjoy this artist’s unique voice and style. Their songs always stand out and are easy to recognize.

Bruno Mars

Bruno Mars is an American singer-songwriter and performer who blends pop, R&B, funk, soul, and reggae influences. His music is known for its groovy rhythms, infectious melodies, and high-energy performances, often paying homage to classic funk and soul while maintaining a contemporary pop sound.This artist’s music gives me comfort and inspiration, especially during difficult times.
